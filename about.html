ABOUT THE CONCEPT

More than a vehicle.

Rayne Motors is a fictional automotive platform designed around premium vehicle discovery, rentals, and a seamless digital browsing experience.

BRAND STORY

Driven by the journey.

The project explores how a modern automotive business could combine vehicle discovery, filtering, rental requests, account experiences, and responsive interfaces in one platform.

Rayne Motors is not a real dealership. There are no real customers, inventory listings, prices, testimonials, or business results represented here.

PROJECT SCOPE

Built to demonstrate the full experience.

01

DISCOVERY

Search, filters, category browsing, editorial gallery and detailed vehicle views.

02

INTERACTION

Cart state, rental validation, navigation, mobile drawer and carousel interactions.

03

RESPONSIVE

Intentional desktop, tablet and mobile layouts with accessible controls and reduced motion support.
